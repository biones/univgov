## Chapter 3: Answering Objections: "Can Rules and Land Truly Be Separated?" and "What About Dictatorships?"

When people encounter UNIVGOV, sharp observers often raise pragmatic counterarguments. We answer them with hard realism rather than wishful thinking.

Minimal, precise rules block abuse without moral paternalism.

### Objection 3: "How could UNIVGOV protect citizens living under brutal totalitarian regimes?"

Fighting regimes that lock citizens in with guns requires asymmetric defense and crypto-capital for physical escape.
